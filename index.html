A riddle from our story

Three Months Until
the Bells Ring

&

Fifteen secrets are waiting. How much you uncover depends on what you know.

Begin the story

In true Linley & Brendan style

How could we throw a party without a quiz worthwhile?
On our wedding website, you can stake your claim,
If you think our minds work one and the same.

We know this quiz may be tricky for some,
So cryptic clues to your rescue now have to come.

Three months remain until vows are spoken,
But first comes a challenge, fifteen secrets unbroken.
Our story is hidden in the verses below,
How much you uncover depends what you know.

Think sideways, read carefully and follow each thread,
The answers are hidden in the story ahead.

Our beginning was delayed before it could begin,

His name belonged to the silver screen, where a licence was his thing.

A familiar name had Brendan talking while Linley ran late,

A famous connection, perhaps, but not the man on our first date.

♡

Then came conversation, laughter and caffeine,

At a place where sitting on the fence would never be seen.

Its name belongs to one committed to a side,

A loyal supporter with allegiance and pride.

♡

Our story soon rose far above the road,

Carrying one nervous man and a carefully hidden load.

We landed where grain once turned beside waters flowing free,

And one question changed the course of what our future came to be.

♡

In our home normal rules sometimes disappear,

And one particular feature still causes raised eyebrows here.

The third porcelain throne sits where no guest would assume,

This place ruled by mise en place, sharp steel and perfume.

♡

Then foreign skies called the three of us away,

To a city where history once changed its name.

Since the guns had fallen still, a new chapter took its place,

Golden years later its people still filled every space.

Flags lined the streets as fire painted the night,

A milestone remembered in colour and light.

♡

Across another ocean, where neon never sleeps,

A city in the sand where every secret keeps.

Dressed naturally in pink, balanced elegantly just so,

A curious choice of name beneath the desert glow.

Among the cards and lights, we found our place to stay,

Its colourful namesake standing tall gave just enough away.

♡

Along the way, small habits began to appear,

Some charming, some puzzling, but all now held dear.

When Linley reaches for sweets, old Noah’s rule must apply.

Though why that ancient custom matters, only Linley knows why.

♡

Hong Kong gave us skylines, markets and remarkable views,

And one shared experience none of us would choose.

Three travellers united by a souvenir unseen,

A rebellion from within that turned us slightly mean.

No postcard or trinket came close to this affair,

Whatever found one of us soon found all three there.

♡

Before our family joined and two became three,

Brendan guarded the moment with patient secrecy.

Nature’s familiar count would still fall one page short.

One further turn of the calendar completes the report.

♡

A playful little journey that ended less than sweet,

When eight little circles making their way towards defeat.

But somewhere along the way, balance lost its charm,

And the night rolled to an ending remembered by one arm.

♡

Later came a birthday and a spotted surprise,

How could i have known she’d be the devil in disguise.

One of his three numbers would mark Aubrey’s demise,

When Luna chose our family, with hatred in her eyes.

♡

Some traditions return when the seasons align,

Queues by design and a crown with no throne,

A yearly Perth pilgrimage our family calls its own.

Through crowds and commotion, we follow the flow,

Returning each year to the place we all know.

♡

Our sporting loyalties divide the turning of each year.

One thrives when summer’s furnace makes the boundaries sear.

The other belongs to winter, where a mooring holds its place,

Two Western names our family proudly choose to embrace.

♡

Then comes the time Brendan waits for all year,

When Bing dreams in white and Bublé reappears.

Three weary travellers still follow a light from afar,

And Mariah wants just one thing, wherever you are.

♡

One final name completes our unusual family tree,

Hidden within the troubled history of hip-hop royalty.

Five dimes placed her after love where there was no loyalty,

A name once carried through a tale of fractured fidelity.

Not born to us, yet claimed as family all the same,

Follow the lyrical trail and you may uncover her name.

The final test

These verses hold the clues, though none will plainly confess;
Some will lead you closer, while others just create a mess.
Fifteen answers await, followed by one final test.
Your best—or worst—marriage advice to decide who knows us best.

Follow the clues and see where they lead,
Some hide in the words, some between what you read.
But answers aside, there’s one thing left to do—
We cannot wait to celebrate this day with you,
Above the Swan, with the city in view,
As the bells ring out and we finally say “I do.”

L & B
